The Basshouj

Two moons, one body

A bass shaped like two moons, in padouk, aluminium and korina.

Body and neckKorina
FingerboardEbony
TopPadouk (red wood), engraved
Rim plateEngraved aluminium, moon phases
NeckTruss rod and two carbon reinforcements, bolted with threaded inserts
NutBone
PickupB-Band
HardwareGotoh Guembri tuners, Apollo Combi Headless, Dunlop Flush Mount strap lock
StringsThree bass strings (65–105) and three guembri strings
